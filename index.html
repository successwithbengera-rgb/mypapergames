Core Pillars
01

Alternating Play

The most important property of any great competitive game is constant back-and-forth. Neither player should sit idle for ten minutes while the other executes a solitaire combo. Every turn should matter to both players.

02

No Non-Games

A non-game is one where the outcome is decided before both players have had a genuine chance to compete. We eliminate blowout elements — not by removing powerful cards, but by ensuring every powerful card has an out. Black Luster Soldier is terrifying. It is not invincible.

03

Win By a Thousand Cuts

Games should be decided by the accumulation of micro-decisions, not by a single turn of execution. Control and midrange archetypes are privileged here because they demand constant adaptation to the board state. No single combo line wins the game. You have to outthink your opponent across every turn.

04

The Expanding Decision Tree

In chess, your gameplan on move 3 becomes irrelevant by move 7. The board demands you adapt. We select formats and matchups that create this dynamic — where your opponent's play forces you to constantly reassess, recalculate, and react. That is where skill lives.
